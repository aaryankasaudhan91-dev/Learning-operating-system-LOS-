# Domain Research: CBSE & NEP 2020 Framework

## Curriculum Structure
The framework defines a multi-term academic cycle (usually 3 terms: April-Aug, Sept-Dec, Jan-March).

### Grade Levels Identified:
- **Class 1-3:** Foundational literacy and numeracy (FLN). Focus on phonics, CVC words, basic operations (CPA approach), and general awareness (EVS).
- **Class 6:** Critical shift to abstract thinking. Introduction of Algebra, Integers, and formal Science disciplines.
- **Class 8:** Advanced algebraic reasoning, spatial geometry, and complex commercial math. Social Science matures into History, Geography, and Civics.
- **Class 10:** Board examination readiness. High-order analytical thinking. Focus on Trigonometry, Analytical Paragraphs, and Geopolitical reasoning.
- **Class 11-12:** Professional curriculum. Higher-order cognitive capacities like Calculus, Vector Algebra, and Organic Chemistry mechanisms.

## Pedagogical Strategies
- **CPA Approach:** Concrete-Pictorial-Abstract for Mathematics.
- **Phonics First:** Sound-based decoding for language acquisition.
- **Micro-Tasking:** Reducing cognitive load through manageable daily tasks.
- **Competency-Based Education (CBE):** Shifting from rote memorization to application and reasoning.

## UI/UX Implications
- The Course Hub must support a variety of content types: lesson plans, syllabus tables, learning objective lists, and instructional flow diagrams.
- Month-by-month syllabus breakdowns are key for teacher/student planning.
- Visual mnemonics and "cheat codes" (e.g., SOH CAH TOA) are emphasized in the framework.
